You might be wondering What is climate Change?

Climate Change

Climate change is the long-term warming of Earth, mostly caused by humans burning fossil fuels, which leads to rising seas, melting ice, and extreme weather.

OH!Thats not all

CAUSES AND EFFECTS

CAUSES!

  1. Burning fossil fuels (coal, oil, and gas)
  2. Deforestation
  3. Industrial farming and livestock
  4. Industrial processes (like cement and steel production)
  5. Waste in landfills

EFFECTS!

  1. Rising global temperatures
  2. Melting glaciers and ice caps
  3. Rising sea levels
  4. More extreme weather (floods, droughts, storms, heatwaves)
  5. Loss of wildlife and ecosystems
  6. Food and water shortages

There is always a solution to this big problem

  • Switch to clean energy
  • Plant trees and protect forests
  • Use less energy and waste less

As the people we need to take action NOW!
